Head of Marketing — Saltgate Analytics
Bristol or remote UK (monthly team days in Bristol)

About Saltgate
Saltgate Analytics builds carbon-accounting software for mid-market manufacturers. We are 60 people, Series A, and growing revenue 80 percent year on year. Marketing today is one junior marketer and an agency retainer; you will be our first marketing leader.

The role
You will own marketing end-to-end: strategy, demand generation, brand, product marketing, and the budget. You will inherit one marketing executive, hire two more people in your first year, and report to the CEO. The near-term mandate is pipeline — sales needs 40 percent of qualified opportunities to come from marketing by the end of next year.

Responsibilities
- Set the marketing strategy and own the annual budget (circa £400k including headcount).
- Build and run the demand-generation engine: campaigns, events, nurture, and the reporting that ties spend to pipeline.
- Own positioning and messaging; run the next website rebuild with an external agency.
- Stand up product marketing: launch narratives, sales enablement, and competitive intelligence for a technical buyer (sustainability and finance leaders in manufacturing).
- Hire, manage, and develop the marketing team as it grows to four.
- Report monthly to the exec team and quarterly to the board on pipeline contribution and CAC.

Required
- 6+ years in B2B marketing with at least 2 years owning demand generation targets tied to pipeline.
- Line-management experience and evidence of developing direct reports.
- Hands-on fluency with a marketing automation platform and CRM reporting (we use HubSpot and Salesforce).
- Strong writing and the judgement to present trade-offs to a CEO and board.

Strongly preferred
- Experience as a first marketing hire or building a function from one or two people.
- Paid-media experience — owning Google Ads and LinkedIn budgets directly, not through an agency.
- Familiarity with sustainability, climate tech, or selling into manufacturing operations.
- Experience commissioning and managing a website rebuild.

We offer £85k–£95k, options, and a learning budget. Interviews are a screening call, a portfolio walk-through of two campaigns you owned, and a working session with the CEO and VP Sales.
