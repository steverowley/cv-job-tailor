Senior Product Manager, Booking — Cardona Health
Remote, UK (occasional travel to London or Manchester)

About Cardona Health
Cardona Health is a venture-backed digital health company. We work with NHS trusts and private clinic groups to digitise patient access — booking, triage, and pre-appointment data capture. We are 130 staff, eight years old, and serve 4.2 million patients across 320 clinical sites in the UK.

The role
We are hiring a Senior Product Manager to own the Booking surface end-to-end. This is the highest-traffic part of our product — 19,000 appointments are booked through it on an average weekday — and it has the most demanding mix of stakeholders, including NHS commissioners, clinical safety officers, and individual practice managers. You will lead a squad of one designer and four engineers.

Responsibilities
- Own the Booking surface roadmap, the discovery work that informs it, and the metrics it is judged against.
- Lead weekly discovery sessions with GPs, practice managers, NHS commissioners, and patients.
- Partner with the Head of Clinical Safety on the DCB0129 safety case for every release.
- Run the cross-functional rituals — sprint planning, refinement, retrospectives — for a squad of five.
- Define and track booking-conversion, abandonment, and time-to-appointment KPIs.

Required
- 3+ years as a product manager owning a customer-facing software product end-to-end.
- Experience running discovery with both end-users and senior internal stakeholders.
- Demonstrated comfort with the metrics craft — defining KPIs, instrumenting them, and reasoning about them weekly.
- Strong written communication, especially PRDs and stakeholder updates.

Strongly preferred
- Health or healthtech background, ideally including the NHS commissioning environment.
- Familiarity with DCB0129 / DCB0160 or other clinical safety standards.
- Background in engineering or design (you have done one of those jobs before becoming a PM).

We offer
£75–95k base, equity, fully remote within the UK with quarterly company offsites. 28 days holiday plus bank holidays, generous L&D budget.
